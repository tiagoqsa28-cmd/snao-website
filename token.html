Frequently Asked Questions

Understand $SNAO.

What is $SNAO?

$SNAO is the blockchain token associated with the SNAO World ecosystem, which includes enterprise software, mobile experiences and crypto intelligence initiatives.

What is the total supply?

The stated fixed maximum supply is 200,000,000 SNAO. No additional minting is planned.

How many tokens are allocated to the Public Sale?

The Public Sale allocation is 45,000,000 SNAO, equivalent to 22.5% of the total supply.

How does progressive pricing work?

The sale is structured into 1,800 lots of 25,000 SNAO, beginning at $0.001 per token. Refer to the official sale page for the current applicable price.

Where can I buy SNAO?

Official purchase information is available at snao.world/sale.html. Always verify the website and transaction details.

Are future token utilities already available?

Not necessarily. Features described as planned or under development are not completed services. Availability depends on actual development and release.

Does ATLAS require SNAO tokens?

ATLAS is being developed as a separate enterprise software product. No mandatory token requirement is established by this page.

Is the founder allocation immediately unlocked?

Under the stated founder vesting plan, 0% unlocks at TGE. A 180-day cliff is followed by 900 days of linear vesting.

Are exchange listings guaranteed?

No. Listings depend on independent exchange decisions, project readiness, technical and liquidity requirements.

Is SNAO a guaranteed investment?

No. Cryptocurrency can be highly volatile. Token ownership does not guarantee profit, liquidity, a future market price or access to unreleased products.
