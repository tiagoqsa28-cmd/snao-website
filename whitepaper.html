SECTION 01

Executive Overview

SNAO World is an evolving technology ecosystem developing enterprise intelligence software, independent mobile digital experiences and crypto market research tools.

The project initially focused on the concept of Ocean Intelligence for cryptocurrency participants. Its broader development direction now includes SNAO World ATLAS, SNAO Ocean and Ocean Intelligence.

ENTERPRISE

SNAO World ATLAS

Intelligent enterprise software for business information, financial analysis, operational insights, risk awareness and automation.

MOBILE

SNAO Ocean

Independent Android and iOS application concept featuring immersive digital experiences, community activities and an internal points system.

CRYPTO INTELLIGENCE

Ocean Intelligence

Research and analytical technology focused on blockchain activity, liquidity, market behavior and risk conditions.

Development Status

SNAO World is developing multiple products. Features described as planned, conceptual or in development are not represented as commercially available.

SECTION 03

Challenges We Aim to Address

Enterprise Complexity

Organizations often use disconnected software for finance, administration, reporting, compliance, operations and security. Fragmentation can increase manual work and reduce visibility.

Information Overload

Large volumes of data do not automatically translate into useful intelligence. Users need context, prioritization and clear explanations.

Unequal Access to Research

Advanced market analysis is frequently distributed across specialized tools, datasets and expensive services. Independent investors may lack integrated information.

Disconnected Digital Communities

Community engagement systems often lack meaningful progression, transparent participation rules and integrated digital experiences.

Our Approach

Develop specialized products for distinct user needs rather than presenting one application as the solution to every problem.

SECTION 06

SNAO Ocean Mobile Application

SNAO Ocean is a planned independent mobile application for Android and iOS, designed around an original immersive ocean environment and structured community participation.

Core Experience

  • Immersive ocean-themed user interface.
  • Account registration and user profiles.
  • Gamified participation sessions.
  • Community missions and eligible activities.
  • Referral and ranking systems.
  • Internal Sardinhas points.
  • Fraud prevention and anti-abuse controls.

Sardinhas Points

Sardinhas are planned as internal application points, not independent blockchain tokens.

The product concept includes periodic eligibility reviews and a potential 30-day conversion cycle involving eligible points and SNAO tokens.

Any conversion mechanism is subject to final rules, eligibility requirements, token availability, technical implementation, anti-fraud validation and applicable regulations.

Not Available Yet

Mining-style activities are planned gamification mechanics. They should not be interpreted as blockchain consensus mining, guaranteed token distribution or guaranteed monetary rewards.

SECTION 12

Security & Responsible Development

Security is a central design consideration across SNAO World products. Different products require different technical and operational safeguards.

Intended Security Practices

  • Authentication and access management appropriate to each application.
  • Data protection and secure processing where applicable.
  • Monitoring and incident-response design.
  • Fraud prevention for community and reward systems.
  • Smart contract testing, review and relevant third-party assessments.
  • Transparent documentation of significant technical risks.

Smart Contract Security

Token, sale, vesting and liquidity-related contracts should be assessed against their intended requirements, with attention to authorization, custody, transaction behavior and user protections.

A planned independent audit must not be represented as completed until its results are available for verification.

Enterprise Data Protection

ATLAS is intended for business environments where access controls, privacy, auditability and system reliability are important. Specific protections must be validated before production use.

Official Sources Only

Never trust private messages claiming to provide privileged sale access, wallet recovery or unofficial contract addresses. Verify information through official SNAO World channels.
