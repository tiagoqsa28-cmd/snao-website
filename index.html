Questions & Answers

Explore SNAO World.

What is SNAO World?

SNAO World is an evolving technology ecosystem focused on enterprise intelligence, digital applications, blockchain research and software innovation.

What is ATLAS Intelligence OS?

ATLAS is an enterprise software initiative under development, intended to connect business information, financial analysis, security awareness and workflow intelligence.

Is SNAO Ocean a Telegram Mini App?

No. SNAO Ocean is being designed as an independent mobile application for Android and iOS.

Are Sardinhas cryptocurrency tokens?

No. Sardinhas are intended as internal participation points within the SNAO Ocean application. Potential reward and conversion mechanisms will depend on defined eligibility rules.

Is the ATLAS platform already released?

ATLAS is in development. Illustrations and concept interfaces are not presented as live production software.

Where can I find information about $SNAO?
