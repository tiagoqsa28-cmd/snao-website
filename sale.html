PROGRESSIVE PRICING

Price Advances by Lot

The sale is structured across 1,800 sequential lots. Each completed lot moves the sale to the next price level.

LOT 1 $0.001 Starting price
LOT 1,800 ≈ $0.221222 Approx. final lot price
25,000 SNAO per lot

A single purchase can cross multiple lots. The total purchase cost therefore depends on the remaining tokens and price of each lot crossed by the transaction.
